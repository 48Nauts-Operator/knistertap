# A little play. A brighter day.

## AI can do more. Let’s keep thinking, too.

Words matter. Attention matters. Thinking for ourselves still matters.

AI can help us write, search, and create. But we still need the vocabulary to express ourselves, the focus to follow an idea, and the judgment to question an answer.

That’s why I’m making Knistertap: simple, playful reasons to give your mind a little work each day. Find a word. Notice a pattern. Try again. A small challenge that belongs to you.

My interest also reaches beyond everyday games. I have been building and exploring apps with the question of whether they could support people living with cognitive difficulties. That question needs careful testing and validation. It is an area I’m exploring, not a claim that these games diagnose or treat a condition.
