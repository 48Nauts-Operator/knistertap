## 4. Wörtli: game data and sharing

Wörtli stores the current daily puzzle day, submitted guesses, game statistics, streak information, and whether you have seen the help screen on your device. This lets the game resume your daily puzzle and display your results. Practice guesses are not saved by the inspected game code.
The current implementation includes Apple Game Center. If you are already authenticated with Game Center, Wörtli can submit your guess count and streak when a daily round ends. Opening the leaderboard is not the only trigger for a submission.
Game Center is an Apple service. Your Game Center profile and scores can be visible to others according to Apple’s service and your settings. Apple describes its handling at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/game-center/. You can manage Game Center through your Apple device settings.
Using the game’s share action sends a result summary to the app or recipient you choose. That destination handles the shared information under its own practices.
